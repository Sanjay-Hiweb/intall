Privacy Policy

Notice: [REPLACE WITH REAL POLICY] — This policy is a legally structured template for the INTALL parametric architectural studio. Consult qualified legal counsel before binding production deployments.

01. Our Minimalist Privacy Philosophy

INTALL is engineered as a local-first architectural drawing tool. We believe your site drawings, plot dimensions, and spatial layouts belong to you. We collect only what is strictly required to maintain your authenticated session and persist your saved revisions.

02. Data Collected via Google Identity Services

When you click "Continue with Google", we request only the minimal standard OpenID Connect scopes:

Field OAuth Scope Technical Purpose
User ID (sub) openid Unique immutable primary key to associate saved CAD drawings.
Email Address email Account identification, permit delivery, and security notices.
Full Name profile Rendering your name on architectural title blocks and sheets.
Profile Photo URL profile Avatar display in the studio toolbar.

We NEVER request access to Google Drive, Contacts, Gmail, or your calendar. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

03. In-Browser CAD Geometry Storage

Floor plan vertices, room polygons, dimension ticks, and 3D meshes are generated and rendered in your browser using client-side JavaScript, Three.js, and SVG. Plans remain in your browser's local memory unless you explicitly click "Save Plan" or export a bill of materials.

04. Cookies & Session Management

We use a single httpOnly, SameSite=Lax session cookie (intall_session) to maintain authentication. We do not use third-party advertising tracking cookies, Facebook Pixels, or cross-site data brokers.

05. Right to Erasure & Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated architectural plans at any time. When you trigger an account deletion request via our studio settings or by submitting a signed request, all records in our database and session files are permanently purged within 60 seconds.
