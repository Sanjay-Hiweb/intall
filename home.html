Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Key specifications regarding parametric scaling, offline security, and architectural utility.

What range of plot dimensions is supported? +
The studio accepts rectangular plots ranging from 15 × 15 ft (225 sqft) up to 100 × 100 ft (10,000 sqft), or metric equivalents (4.5m to 30.5m). Both standard proportions and long urban narrow parcels are mathematically accommodated.
Does this software replace a licensed architect? +
No. Aether Design is an instant architectural conceptualization and spatial exploration studio. It empowers landowners, builders, and developers to explore room configurations before engaging licensed architects and structural engineers for municipal approval drawings.
How does the parametric geometric engine adapt rooms? +
Every archetypal floor plan is authored against a reference plot dimension. When you input your plot size, independent X and Y scale factors adapt each room while checking that rooms never fall below habitable building standards.
Can floor plans be exported for CAD and contractor collaboration? +
Yes. You can export high-resolution PNG blueprints for visual alignment or download a portable JSON state file. You can import this JSON state file back into the app at any time to resume editing.
Are designs uploaded to an external server? +
No. All computations, 2D vector drawing, and 3D Three.js WebGL scenes execute entirely client-side in your browser. Your property dimensions and designs remain completely private.
