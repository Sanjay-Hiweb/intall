§ 07 / CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verifiable technical answers about our drafting solver, export standards, and data security.

01 How does the parametric solver adapt to non-standard or narrow plots? +
INTALL uses a parametric constraint solver. When you adjust width or depth, room boundaries don't simply stretch as a bitmap; rooms scale proportionally along independent X and Y structural axes, automatically redistributing circulation corridors and preserving minimum legal bedroom dimensions.
02 Which building codes and municipal bye-laws are audited? +
The automated compliance engine checks against the Model Building Bye-Laws and the National Building Code of India (NBC 2016) residential standards. It verifies front/rear setbacks, maximum permissible ground coverage (typically 70%), minimum habitable room sizes (9.5 m²), corridor clearances (≥0.90 m), and natural ventilation window ratios.
03 Can I export vector CAD files into AutoCAD, Revit, or SketchUp? +
Yes. The Studio exports clean, layered vector DXF and SVG geometry that you can immediately import into AutoCAD, ArchiCAD, Revit, or Rhino. All wall lines, door swings, and dimension strings exist on dedicated, standard CAD layers.
04 Are plot dimensions and layout drawings stored on external servers? +
No. INTALL runs 100% client-side inside your browser engine. Your plot measurements, room layouts, and design revisions never leave your local device memory unless you explicitly click to save or share.
05 Does the solver support Vastu Shastra principles and solar orientation? +
Yes. The solver incorporates cardinal quadrant mapping (North, East, South, West ingress) to position culinary zones in the Agni (Southeast) quadrant, master suites in Nairuti (Southwest), and water/open areas in Ishanya (Northeast).
06 What hardware or browser is required to run the studio? +
INTALL is optimized for instant startup (<2 seconds) on any modern laptop, iPad, or mobile phone. The 2D drafting engine uses lightweight vector canvas, and the 3D WebGL orbit viewport engages hardware acceleration smoothly even on integrated graphics.
